# Wrap up

The one habit this brain asks of them. It takes two seconds from their side, and everything
after that is your job.

Do not ask them questions. Do the work and show them what you wrote.

## 1. Check the disk, not your memory of the conversation

Look at what actually changed in the folder this session. What you remember writing and what
is on disk are not always the same thing.

## 2. Write today's page

`Today/` — one page for today, named by its date. If one already exists, add to it.

- What happened and what was said.
- What got decided.
- Where each live thing got to, and what it is waiting on.
- Anything they said they want, doubt, or are weighing up — in their own words. **This is the
  most valuable thing in any session and the easiest to lose.**

## 3. Put things where they belong

Anything decided goes into `Decisions/` with the date and the reason. Anything about a person
goes on their page in `People/`. Anything that stays true goes into `Knowledge/`. Anything
finished moves to `Done/`. Clear `Inbox/`.

## 4. Update `About me.md`

If they told you something about themselves today, it goes in, in their own words. If it
answers a blank, replace the waiting line — do not leave it sitting underneath their answer.

## 5. Update `Catalogue.md`

One line for every file you made today.

## 6. Tell them, in four lines or fewer

What you wrote down. What is still open. What is waiting on them. What you would pick up
first next time.

## Never

- Never write down something they did not say.
- Never mark anything as done because it was discussed. Done means finished.
- Never leave the session without writing today's page. If nothing happened, write that.
